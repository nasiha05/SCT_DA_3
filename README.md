# HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Dashboard

An interactive HR Analytics Dashboard developed using Power BI to explore employee attrition, workforce characteristics, and factors associated with employee turnover.

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing employee data and presenting the results through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard allows users to explore employee attrition across different departments, age groups, job roles, salary levels, gender, overtime status, job satisfaction, and work experience.

## Objectives

- Analyse overall employee attrition
- Measure the number and rate of employees who left
- Compare attrition across departments and job roles
- Examine attrition patterns across different age groups
- Analyse the relationship between overtime and attrition
- Explore job satisfaction and employee turnover
- Examine attrition across salary levels and work experience
- Provide an interactive dashboard for HR-focused analysis

## Dataset

The project uses the HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset.

The dataset contains employee-level information including:

- Age
- Department
- Job Role
- Gender
- Business Travel
- Distance From Home
- Education
- Job Satisfaction
- Monthly Income
- Overtime
- Performance Rating
- Total Experience
- Years at Company
- Years in Current Role
- Attrition
- and other employee-related attributes

Dataset file:

`Dataset/HR_Analytics-4.csv`

## Dashboard Features

The Power BI dashboard includes:

- Total Employees
- Active Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Average Experience
- Attrition by Department
- Attrition by Salary Slab
- Attrition by Job Role
- Age Group Distribution
- Attrition by Gender
- Attrition by Experience
- Department-wise Employee Distribution

Interactive filters are provided for areas such as:

- Department
- Age Group
- Job Role
- Overtime

These filters allow the dashboard visuals and key metrics to update dynamically.

## Key Analysis Areas

### Department Analysis

The dashboard compares employee attrition across different departments to identify areas with different levels of employee turnover.

### Age Analysis

Employees are grouped into age ranges to examine how attrition varies across different stages of the workforce.

### Job Role Analysis

Attrition is compared across individual job roles to identify roles with different employee turnover patterns.

### Overtime Analysis

The dashboard examines employee attrition in relation to overtime status.

### Job Satisfaction

Job satisfaction levels are compared with employee attrition to explore differences in turnover across satisfaction levels.

### Experience Analysis

Employee experience and years at the company are analysed to understand how attrition varies across different experience levels.
